Privacy Policy

Frizz · Last updated 21 August 2026

This policy explains what information the Frizz mobile application ("Frizz", "the app") collects, why it is collected, and what control you have over it. Frizz is provided by 33 FROGS PTY LTD ("we", "us").

The short version. Frizz stores the financial records you choose to enter, plus the email address you sign up with. We do not run analytics or advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing purposes.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. Your password is never stored in readable form and is not visible to us.

Information you enter into the app

Frizz is a personal and business finance tracker, so its core function is storing what you record in it:

CategoryWhat it includes
BusinessesBusiness names and related details you create
TransactionsAmount, type (income or expense), description, and date
SavingsSavings goals, savings rules, and contributions toward them
IdeasNotes and ideas you save in the app

This content is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account and is retrievable only by you when signed in.

Information kept only on your device

  • App lock PIN, if you set one, and your biometric-unlock preference. These are saved in your device's local app storage and are never transmitted to us.
  • Face ID / Touch ID. Biometric verification is performed entirely by iOS. Frizz never receives, sees, or stores your fingerprint or face data — only a success or failure result.

Technical information

Firebase processes limited operational data needed to run the service securely, such as IP address, device and operating-system type, and timestamps of sign-in attempts. This is used for authentication, abuse prevention, and reliability.

2. What we do not collect

  • No analytics or usage-tracking SDKs. Firebase Analytics is disabled in this app.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers, or third-party ad networks.
  • No tracking of your activity across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No location data, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No sale or rental of personal information to third parties.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and authenticate your account and keep you signed in.
  • To store, sync, and display the financial records you enter.
  • To generate the PDF and CSV reports you request.
  • To keep the service secure and to diagnose technical faults.
  • To respond to you if you contact support.

We do not use your financial records for profiling, marketing, or automated decision-making.

4. Legal bases for processing

33 FROGS PTY LTD is an Australian company and handles personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR also applies, we rely on: performance of a contract to provide the app's core functionality to you; legitimate interests to secure the service and prevent abuse; and your consent where it is specifically requested. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Reports and sharing you initiate

Frizz can export your records as PDF or CSV files. Those exports are generated on your device, and they go only where you send them using the iOS share sheet — email, files, printing, or another app of your choice. Once you share a file, this policy no longer governs it; the receiving service's terms apply.

6. Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited) for authentication and database hosting. Firebase processes data on our behalf under Google's terms and may store it on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google applies Standard Contractual Clauses for such transfers. See Firebase's privacy documentation.

We use no other third-party processors.

7. Data retention

Your account information and records are retained for as long as your account exists. When your account is deleted, the associated records are deleted from our active systems. Residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period before being overwritten.

8. Deleting your account and data

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing mkfrizziero@gmail.com from the address registered to your account. We will action the request within 30 days.

Data held only on your device — your PIN and unlock preference — is removed when you delete the app.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your data, correct inaccurate data, delete your data, restrict or object to processing, receive your data in a portable format, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Residents of California may additionally request disclosure of categories of information collected and are protected against discrimination for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these, contact mkfrizziero@gmail.com. Note that the app's built-in PDF and CSV export already gives you a portable copy of your financial records at any time.

Australian users may complain to us in the first instance, and if unsatisfied with our response, to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS) and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest. Access to your records requires your account credentials. You can add an app-level PIN or biometric lock in Settings for additional protection on a shared device.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password.

11. Children

Frizz is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "last updated" date at the top will be revised, and material changes will be communicated in the app or by email where appropriate.
